## 1. Topical Authority Framework Overview

Search engines like Google rank websites that demonstrate comprehensive, authoritative depth across an entire topic rather than isolated keywords. The **Topic Cluster Model** connects a central, authoritative **Pillar Page** to specialized **Cluster Content (Spokes)** using deliberate, bidirectional internal links.

FestiveWala organizes content into two complementary cluster dimensions:
1. **Festival-Centric Topic Clusters:** Vertically focused on specific celebrations (e.g. The Complete Diwali Authority Cluster).
2. **Product-Centric Experience Hubs:** Horizontally focused on functional celebration needs across all festivals (Wishes Hub, Greeting Studio, Rangoli Gallery, Decor Hub, Gift Hub, Guides Hub).
