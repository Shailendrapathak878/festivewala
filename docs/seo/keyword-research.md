---

## 1. Research Methodology & Governance Principles

Indian festival search behavior is uniquely multilingual, culturally nuanced, and seasonal. Search queries oscillate between pure English, formal Devanagari Hindi, and conversational Roman Hindi (Hinglish) depending on the user's demographic, device, context, and immediate need.

### Strict Data Integrity Policy
- **No Fabricated Metrics:** Search volumes, keyword difficulty scores, and cost-per-click values from third-party tools (Ahrefs, Semrush) are **not fabricated or estimated without audited empirical telemetry**. FestiveWala is pre-launch and operates on a ₹0 budget.
- **Intent-Driven Qualitative Analysis:** Queries are evaluated based on verified linguistic syntax, seasonal search intent, cultural celebration cycles, and actual user phrasing patterns observed across Indian messaging and search platforms.
- **Topical Clustering:** Similar queries with identical intent are grouped into unified landing page destinations to avoid thin doorway pages or keyword cannibalization.
