## 1. Editorial Core Values & E-E-A-T Framework

FestiveWala operates at the intersection of cultural celebration, emotional family connection, and practical festive preparation. Content directly influences how families celebrate, prepare sacred rituals, and honor traditions.

Every article, greeting message, guide, and FAQ must adhere to Google's **E-E-A-T Guidelines** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness):

1. **Experience:** Content must reflect real-world celebration practicalities (e.g. how long gulal takes to wash out, which clay diyas absorb less oil, how to manage clutter before Diwali).
2. **Expertise:** Written with deep cultural fluency, understanding the nuances of regional traditions across India (Bengal's Kali Puja on Diwali, Mathura's Lathmar Holi, South Indian Kolams).
3. **Authoritativeness:** Clear attribution, structured data schemas, and clean cross-linking establishing FestiveWala as an authoritative celebration platform.
4. **Trustworthiness:** Transparent claims, zero clickbait, zero fabricated astrological promises, and respect for user privacy (no data tracking or photo harvesting).

## 2. Linguistic Standards Across Three Languages

### 2.1 English Content Standards
- **Tone:** Warm, celebratory, refined, culturally literate, and welcoming to all generations.
- **Vocabulary:** Rich yet accessible. Balance poetic elegance with modern clarity.
- **Formatting:** Use clean section headings, scannable bullet points, time badges, and concise takeaway summaries.
- **Forbidden:** Overly corporate jargon, dry bureaucratic prose, or Westernized clichés that dilute authentic Indian cultural context.

### 2.2 Hindi (Devanagari) Content Standards
- **Typography & Encoding:** 100% valid UTF-8 Unicode characters. Ensure correct rendering of conjunct consonants (संयुक्ताक्षर) and proper matras (मात्राएं).
- **Tone & Phrasing:** Natural, respectful, conversational, and culturally elevated (सात्विक, आत्मीय व सुरुचिपूर्ण).
- **Pronouns:** Use respectful pronouns (*आप*, *आपके*, *अपनों*) when addressing the reader.
- **Clarity:** Avoid mechanical, overly literal machine translations that sound awkward to native Hindi speakers (e.g. translating "drop-in" literally).
- **Poetic Meter:** In wishes and captions, preserve natural rhythm (छंद, तुक व लय).

### 2.3 Hinglish (Roman Hindi) Content Standards
Hinglish is the informal language of over 500 million Indian smartphone users. It must reflect how real people speak with friends and family on WhatsApp and search on Google.

#### The Golden Rules of Hinglish at FestiveWala:
1. **Colloquial & Natural Phrasing:**
   - *Good:* "Diwali par kam budget me ghar ki safai aur sundar decoration kaise karein?"
   - *Bad:* "Kaise saaf karein ghar Diwali ke liye kam kharch me" (unnatural syntax).
2. **Harmonious Vocabulary:** Use familiar Hindi vocabulary written in clean Roman phonetic spelling.
   - *Good:* "Bhai-behen ke atoot pyaar aur suraksha ka pavitra tyohar."
   - *Bad:* Randomly injecting English grammar with disjointed Hindi words.
3. **Contextual Placement:** Weave Hinglish naturally into FAQs, regional aliases, filter search terms, and social caption suggestions. **Never** artificially force Roman Hindi into formal historical or religious articles.

## 3. Cultural & Factual Verification Guidelines

1. **Calendar Tithis & Muhurats:**
   - All tithi statements (e.g., *Kartik Amavasya*, *Phalguna Purnima*, *Shravana Purnima*) must correspond to verified Vedic Panchang astronomical calendars.
   - Muhurat time ranges (e.g. Pradosh Kaal, Nishita Kaal) must be clearly stated as standard traditional windows, noting that local sunrise/sunset times vary slightly by city.
2. **Mythological Lore & Historical Context:**
   - Reference recognized scriptural sources (Skanda Purana, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Padma Purana) when explaining traditions.
   - Distinct regional traditions must be acknowledged with respect (e.g. Kali Puja in Bengal during Diwali; Govardhan Puja in North India; Deepavali oil bath in Tamil Nadu).
3. **Safety & Health Instructions:**
   - In DIY recipes and craft guides (such as herbal colors or flower rangoli), specify non-toxic ingredients (cornstarch, arrowroot, turmeric). Provide explicit skin sensitivity notes and avoid suggesting harsh chemical additives.

## 4. Anti-Spam & Content Integrity Rules

- **Zero Plagiarism:** All wishes, guides, descriptions, and FAQs must be 100% original. Never copy competitor greeting websites or scrapers.
- **No Keyword Stuffing:** A search term must never be repeated awkwardly to manipulate search algorithms. If a keyword cannot be inserted with grammatical grace, it must not be inserted.
- **No AI Filler Content:** Avoid generic, verbose fluff sentences (e.g., *"Festivals are celebrated in India by many people because they are very nice and bring happiness to everyone everywhere"*). Every paragraph must provide concrete knowledge, practical advice, or emotional resonance.
- **Transparent FAQ Format:** FAQs must answer the core question directly in the first 2 sentences, followed by helpful context or practical steps.
