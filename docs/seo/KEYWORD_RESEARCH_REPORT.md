## 1. Executive Summary & Market Landscape

Search behavior around Indian festivals exhibits unique seasonal and cultural characteristics:
1. **Hyper-Personalization Demand:** Broad queries (e.g., "diwali wishes") are rapidly shifting toward action-oriented, personalized search phrases such as *"photo wala diwali card"*, *"apne naam ka greeting card"*, and *"free online festival greeting card maker"*.
2. **Extreme Cyclical Spikes (Q4 Peak):** Traffic surges by 500% to 2,000% between Dhanteras, Diwali, Govardhan Puja, and Bhai Dooj.
3. **Multilingual Tri-Intent:** Users search interchangeably in English, conversational Romanized Hindi ("Hinglish"), and pure Hindi Devanagari script.
4. **Mobile & WhatsApp Dominance:** High search intent for instant 1-click sharing, 9:16 vertical statuses, and lightweight download formats without watermarks.

## 3. SEO Strategy Implications

1. **Avoid Extreme Generic Keyword Traps:** Targeting single-word terms like "Diwali" is unrealistic against Wikipedia, Britannica, and major news media. FestiveWala instead targets high-intent, action-oriented clusters (*"Dhanteras wishes and greetings"*, *"free online greeting card maker"*, *"simple rangoli designs for diwali"*).
2. **Prevent Internal Keyword Cannibalization:**
   - `/festivals/diwali` targets informational, cultural, and ritual search intent (*"Diwali 5 days significance and traditions"*).
   - `/wishes/diwali` targets social copy-paste and status intent (*"Diwali wishes and quotes in Hindi and English"*).
   - `/greetings/diwali` targets card creation and design download (*"Diwali greeting card maker"*).
3. **Structured Data Amplification:** Every cluster maps to matching Schema.org types (`WebSite`, `WebApplication`, `CollectionPage`, `Article`, `FAQPage`) to compete for Google SERP Rich Snippets.
