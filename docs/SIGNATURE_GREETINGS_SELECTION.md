## 2. Selection Framework

Each candidate template was evaluated on:

| Criterion | Weight | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Visual attractiveness** | 25% | Palette, depth, emotional impact |
| **Typography quality** | 20% | Font choice, size, language readability |
| **Layout & composition** | 20% | Balance, hierarchy, negative space |
| **Color harmony** | 15% | Palette originality & internal coherence |
| **Originality & uniqueness** | 10% | Avoids repetition across the 20 selected |
| **Personalization potential** | 10% | Named recipient, occasion adaptability |

**Design diversity mandate:** The 20 must span at least 6 distinct visual directions — no more than 5 from any single category.

- **5 distinct fonts**: Cinzel (regal), Playfair Display (classical), Rozha One (Indian heritage), Marcellus (editorial), Cinzel (executive)

- **3 background gradient types**: Radial, Linear vertical, Solid

> [!TIP]
> **Recommended display order** in the featured section: Start with the most visually striking designs (sovereign-lotus, royal-diya, traditional-rangoli) then alternate between warm, cool, and neutral palettes for the best browsing experience.
