# FestiveWala — Brand Guidelines (Phase 1)

**Tagline:** *Celebrate. Create. Share.*  
**Hindi:** *उत्सव • सृजन • आनंद*  
**Mission:** To elevate Indian festival celebrations into enduring digital art through bespoke greeting cards, cultural poetry, and refined craftsmanship.

---

## 1. Creative Vision & Brand Philosophy

FestiveWala sits at the intersection of **cultural reverence** and **international editorial design**. It reinterprets Indian festival traditions—Diwali, Holi, Raksha Bandhan, Navratri, Eid, and more—not through kitsch, clip-art, or generic festive banners, but with the dignity, restraint, and tactile elegance of a luxury publisher.

### What FestiveWala IS:
* **Celebratory & Joyful:** Reflects the genuine warmth and light of Indian homes during festivities.
* **Culturally Grounded:** Respects historical motifs, Vedic shlokas, and authentic phrasing in Hindi and English.
* **Modern & Editorial:** Uses generous whitespace, refined serifs, clean geometry, and high-performance modern web architecture.
* **Tactile & Bespoke:** Gives digital greetings the emotional weight and visual richness of embossed gold foil cards on handmade ivory paper.

### What FestiveWala is NOT:
* Not a cluttered festival blog stuffed with aggressive ads.
* Not a generic WordPress/Bootstrap festival site.
* Not an impersonal SaaS dashboard.
* Not a barrage of neon gradients and repetitive card grids.
* Not mechanical machine translations that butcher Hindi grammar or nuance.

## 2. Color Palette & Distribution Rules

The FestiveWala palette is strictly codified to guarantee visual depth, sophistication, and contrast across light and dark modes.

| Color Token | Hex Code | Visual Character | Role & Distribution Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deep Purple** | `#24113D` | Regal, nocturnal, sacred | **Depth & Contrast.** Used for primary dark backgrounds, footers, typography headlines, and rich structural anchors. |
| **Royal Purple** | `#45216B` | Sovereign, majestic | **Atmospheric Depth.** Used for subtle layered cards, active drawer items, and gradient accents. Never applied as loud full-screen backgrounds. |
| **Warm Ivory** | `#FAF7F0` | Parchment, breathable, tactile | **Primary Canvas.** The default body background. Provides breathing room and eliminates harsh sterile pure-white glare. |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | Crisp, clean | **Card Surfaces.** Keeps card modules and interactive surfaces crisp and legible. |
| **Champagne Gold**| `#F2D98B` | Luminous, candlelit | **Light Highlight.** Used for diya flames, subtle text glows, and secondary buttons. |
| **Metallic Gold** | `#D4AF37` | 24K Leaf, auspicious | **Premium Accents.** Used for borders, icons, decorative rangoli lines, and primary callouts. Never overused to the point of gaudiness. |
| **Text Dark** | `#21182A` | Deep charcoal aubergine | **High Legibility Body Text.** Never harsh pitch-black (`#000000`). |
| **Muted Text** | `#766D7D` | Warm neutral grey | **Secondary Labels & Meta.** Used for dates, categories, and breadcrumbs. |
| **Light Purple** | `#F3ECFA` | Petal tint | **Soft Interactive Badges.** Used for pill backgrounds and subtle hover states. |

### Color Composition Rules:
1. **60-30-10 Rule:** 60% Warm Ivory / Pure White canvas, 30% Deep Purple structural contrast, 10% Gold highlights.
2. **No Monolithic Purple Gradients:** Gradient backgrounds must be strictly reserved for high-impact hero cards or spotlight sections.
3. **Gold as Shimmer, Not Paint:** Gold borders are delicate (`0.75px` to `1.5px`), never thick heavy frames.

## 3. Brand Voice and Editorial Tone

* **Poetic yet Accessible:** Balances classic elegance (*"Illuminating traditions with luminous artistry"*) with modern clarity.
* **Culturally Reverent:** Honors Sanskrit shlokas and Hindi idioms with authentic context and natural phrasing.
* **Warm and Welcoming:** Treats every visitor like an honored guest arriving for a festive gathering (*"Atithi Devo Bhava"*).

---

## 4. Logo Usage Guidelines

The FestiveWala brand emblem combines:
1. A **sacred clay diya base** rendered in metallic gold gradient.
2. A **radiant golden flame** with an inner white luminous core.
3. An **outer dashed celestial halo** symbolizing unity, eternity, and celebratory radiance.
4. An **editorial serif wordmark** set in `Cinzel` / `Playfair Display`.

### Clear Space & Minimum Sizing:
* **Minimum Digital Height:** 28px.
* **Clear Space:** Equal to half the width of the emblem on all sides.
* **Variations:** Dark (for light ivory canvas), Light (for deep purple canvas), and Gold Gradient (for luxury hero cards).
